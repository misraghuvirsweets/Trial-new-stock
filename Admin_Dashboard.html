Expected Closing = Opening Stock (yesterday's closing) + Dispatch Received (today) − Sales Sold (today), compared against the Actual Closing your branch staff enter on the Ram Mandir / Vishrambagh / Miraj sheet tabs. This Actual Closing is also what powers the Stock & Valuation tabs' "Opening Stock" figure — there's no separate opening-stock entry step anymore.
